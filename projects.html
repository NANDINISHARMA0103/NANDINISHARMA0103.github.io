Banking Sector NPA Analysis

02 / 03

How did non-performing assets evolve across Indian banking sectors?

Analysed 27 years of banking data from 1997–2024 to compare non-performing assets across Public Sector Banks and Private Banks.

The analysis used OLS regression alongside Newey-West HAC errors and a range of time-series diagnostics.
